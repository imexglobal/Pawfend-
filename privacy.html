8. 위치

위치정보의 이용 조건은 이용약관 제18조에 있습니다. 이 버전에서 회사는 개인위치정보를 서버에 저장하지 않습니다. 따라서 개인위치정보의 보유 기간, 제3자 제공, 이용·제공사실 확인자료는 없습니다. 휴대폰 위치 권한을 거절하면 실시간 GPS만 꺼지고 앱의 나머지 기능은 사용할 수 있습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-23 · Controller IMEX Global · Privacy officer: the representative of IMEX Global · hellopawfend@gmail.com

The Korean text controls if this English text differs. This policy lists only what this version of PetNeighbor actually handles.

1. Who handles personal information

The controller is IMEX Global. The privacy officer is the representative of IMEX Global, at hellopawfend@gmail.com. The service introduces neighbors for pet care and provides chat. The company is not a party to the care arrangement.

2. Before an account exists

Until a Supabase project is connected, the app is a demo. Profiles, posts, bookings, chats, and consent ticks stay on that device and are not sent to an IMEX Global server. Opening a map loads OpenStreetMap tiles. Fonts and the map and account libraries load from jsDelivr and cdnjs. Those requests carry the device’s IP address. The demo does not create an account.

3. Items, purposes, and grounds

After the operator connects Supabase and a member signs up, the items below are processed. The grounds are article 15 of the Personal Information Protection Act: contract performance for what the service needs (article 15(1)(4)), and legitimate interests for reports and blocks that protect other members (article 15(1)(6)). The location-terms checkbox is a separate consent (article 15(1)(1)), but this version does not collect the coordinates themselves.

4. Not uploaded in this version

  • Community posts and comments, place recommendations and ratings, care requests, applications, bookings, reviews, and walk routes stay on the device. They disappear from that device when the app is removed or demo data is reset.
  • GPS during a walk or visit is used only to draw the route on that device. It is not sent to the company or to the other member. The app does not ask for location until care starts. Refusing the phone permission leaves the rest of an existing account usable.
  • Language choice stays on the device.

5. Retention and destruction

When the purpose ends, the information is destroyed without delay. Account information is kept until the member deletes the account, and it is destroyed then. In the app, Me → Account → Delete account deletes the auth user and, with it, the profile, pets, conversations the member was in (including the other person’s copy of that thread), messages, push registrations, consent records, blocks, and the reporter link on reports that member filed. A report someone else filed about this member may remain, with the reported-person field cleared, until the operator deletes it within one year of the report. If the member cannot sign in, they can email hellopawfend@gmail.com from the account address and ask for deletion.

Electronic files are deleted so they are not restored. The company does not keep personal information on paper.
